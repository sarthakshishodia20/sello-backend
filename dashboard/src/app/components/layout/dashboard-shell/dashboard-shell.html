Understanding the Sello Ecosystem

A comprehensive admin guide for managing merchants, products, orders, and analytics on Sello.

System Architecture
Masterbrand:

The global identity that controls all settings, master catalogues, and universal categories. Changes here affect all linked merchants automatically.

Merchant:

Independent business units under a Masterbrand. They manage their own orders, customers, and store-specific products with local pricing overrides.

Customer Storefront:

The public-facing webapp where customers browse products, place orders, and track delivery — linked directly to a Merchant's catalogue.

Admin Dashboard:

This panel — giving Masterbrand admins full visibility across all merchants, a consolidated order view, analytics, and product management.

Catalog & Core Features Guide

A comprehensive breakdown of Sello's advanced catalog tools, modular merchant extensions, visibility layers, and sorting mechanisms.

Product Drag & Swap Sort Merchant Only

What it is: A visual drag-and-drop mechanism to instantly arrange products display sequence on the storefront mobile webapp.

How it works: Drag items up/down. Sorting persists on storefront refresh. Master Admin cannot sort, ensuring clean master catalogues.

Sello Extensions System Master Config

What it is: A suite of modular features configured by Masterbrand Admin globally in the Top Shell Navbar for merchants:

  • Out of Stock: Allow merchants to snooze products locally.
  • Custom Brand: Inherit brand items and override prices/copy.
  • COD Toggle: Turn on cash-on-delivery store checkout.
  • Floating Icons: Toggle live support bubbles on storefront.
  • Notification Sounds: Enable order alert tones for merchant dashboards.
  • Marketplace Availability: Let merchants set weekly opening hours — stores auto-activate/deactivate based on the schedule.
Out of Stock (Snooze) Merchant Toggled

What it is: Instantly snooze ordering on products. The item remains visible for customer storefront branding but hides checkout and adds an "Out of Stock" warning.

Gemini AI Copywriter Both Roles

What it is: Tap the sparkles button in the edit/create drawer. Gemini AI automatically drafts high-conversion marketing copy based on title & category.

Dual Visibility Toggle Both Roles

What it is: Double-layered visibility. Masterbrand Admin controls global active state. Linked merchants inherit this, but can disable local storefront visibility if permitted.

Calendar Date Search Both Roles

What it is: Select date ranges from the calendar to instantly search, filter, and audit logs, catalogue updates, and order history chronologically.

Marketplace Availability

A weekly schedule system that lets merchant admins define exactly which days and hours their store is open — the platform then auto-enables or disables their store in real time.

Step 1 — Enable via Extension Master Admin

Open the Extensions panel (tag icon in the top navbar) and toggle Marketplace Availability to ON. This immediately shows the Availability page in every Merchant Admin's sidebar.

Step 2 — Configure Weekly Schedule Merchant Admin

Go to Availability in the sidebar. Enable Custom Availability Schedule, then toggle each day Open/Closed. For days that should have limited hours, switch from Open All Day to Specific Time and add one timeslot.

Auto Store Activation Automated

How it works: A background job runs every 60 seconds and evaluates each merchant's schedule against the current day and time. The store is automatically set Active when open and Inactive when closed — no manual action needed.

Real-time checks also run whenever the dashboard fetches merchant profiles or the public storefront loads a store page.

Manual Toggle Restriction Master Admin

Important: If a merchant's schedule marks them as closed right now, the Merchants table toggle will not allow you to force-enable them. A warning will appear: "Delete the timeslot to manually enable."

To override the schedule and force-enable a store, the merchant must go to Availability, remove the conflicting timeslot, and then the toggle will work normally.

Product Status Filter Both Roles

Bonus feature: The Products page now has a Show Items dropdown below the search bar. Filter between All Products, Enabled Only, or Disabled Only — works for both Master Admin and Merchant Admin views.

One Timeslot Per Day

Each day supports exactly one timeslot (e.g. 09:00 – 18:00). Click Edit Timeslot on any day row to open the dialog — you'll see a tip reminding you of this limit. Outside these hours, the store is automatically deactivated.

Order Lifecycle

Every order goes through distinct stages from placement to delivery. Use the Status dropdown in the Orders table to update the current stage.

PLACED

New order received from customer storefront. Awaiting merchant acknowledgment.

CONFIRMED

Merchant has accepted and acknowledged the order. Inventory is now reserved.

PREPARING

Order is being processed or packed for dispatch by the merchant's team.

DISPATCHED

Handled over to delivery partner; out for delivery to the customer's address.

DELIVERED

Order successfully received and confirmed by the customer. Revenue counted.

CANCELLED

Order rejected by merchant or cancelled by customer before dispatch.

Merchant Management
Create Merchant:

Use the + button in the Merchants page to onboard a new business. They'll receive login credentials and have their own isolated catalogue.

Active / Inactive Toggle:

Deactivate a merchant to temporarily hide their storefront and prevent new orders, without deleting any data.

Catalogue Preview:

Click any Merchant ID to open a side panel showing their inherited product catalogue with current prices and availability.

Revenue Tracking:

Each merchant's detail card shows total delivered revenue — only orders with DELIVERED status count towards revenue.

Analytics Dashboard
Order Trends:

Visualizes order volume over time across all merchants, helping identify peak days and seasonal patterns.

Revenue Breakdown:

Aggregated revenue split by merchant, period, and payment method. Includes delivered-only totals for accurate financials.

Top Products:

Shows the most frequently ordered products across all stores, identifying bestsellers for promotional focus.

Custom Audio Alerts Engine
Personalized Chime Preferences Both Roles

What it is: A premium, multi-tenant earcon sound notification system. Masterbrand Admins can toggle the sound module globally in the extensions menu. Merchants can then select from 5 snappy chimes (under 1 second), preview the tones, and save them.

Dashboard Specific Triggers Merchant Only

When it plays: To maintain a clean and distraction-free workspace, dashboard chime notifications play **ONLY** during these two events:

  • Order Status Updates: Plays immediately when you change the status of any order (e.g. Accept, Complete, Decline) in the Orders tab.
  • Safety Delete/Archive Alerts: Triggers a sound warning whenever products, categories, or customers are deleted or archived across the dashboard.
Storefront Checkout Success Merchant Only

When it plays: Plays your personalized merchant sound directly to the customer at the exact millisecond their order checkout succeeds on the storefront website! No audio is triggered on add-to-cart clicks, preserving a premium shopping experience.
